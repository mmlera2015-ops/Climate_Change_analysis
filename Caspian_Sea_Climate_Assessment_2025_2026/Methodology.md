# Methodology and Data Limitations

## Assessment Period and Study Area

The assessment covers the 2025–2026 water year, from 1 October 2025 to 30 September 2026. It uses observations from eight selected synoptic stations along the southern Caspian Sea coast:

- Anzali
- Ramsar
- Nowshahr
- Babolsar
- Sari
- Torkaman
- Bandar-e Gaz
- Amirabad

## Variables

The report examines:

- Mean air temperature
- Mean maximum air temperature
- Mean minimum air temperature
- Precipitation
- Evaporation

Relative humidity is not included in the current quantitative results and should not be described as an assessed variable unless its station data and results are added.

## Comparison with Climatological Normals

Observed values are compared with corresponding climatological normals. Anomalies are calculated as:

`Anomaly = Observed value − Climatological normal`

For temperature variables, annual-period means are compared. For precipitation and evaporation, annual-period accumulations are compared.

Percentage departures, where reported, are calculated relative to the corresponding normal.

## Treatment of Missing Evaporation Data

For months without available observed evaporation data, the corresponding monthly climatological normal was used to complete the annual series. Consequently, some annual evaporation totals combine observed values and climatological substitutions.

This limitation is important: these totals are not entirely observation-based, and the resulting anomalies should be interpreted cautiously. The number and identity of substituted months should be documented for each station in the final technical report.

## Interpretation and Limitations

- The arithmetic mean of the eight station values is a simple station average, not an area-weighted coastal or basin-wide estimate.
- The negative minimum-temperature anomalies occurring alongside positive mean- and maximum-temperature anomalies require further verification. Original observations, missing-data treatment, station consistency, reference periods, and annual aggregation methods should be checked before drawing conclusions.
- Evaporation measured or estimated at coastal meteorological stations should not be equated with evaporation from the Caspian Sea surface.
- Station-based climate anomalies alone cannot establish the cause or magnitude of Caspian Sea level changes. A complete water-balance assessment also requires river inflow, precipitation over the sea, and sea-surface evaporation.

## Quality-Control Checklist Before Final Publication

1. Verify the minimum-temperature observations and calculations at all eight stations.
2. Confirm that the same climatological reference period and calculation conventions were used for each station and variable.
3. Document missing observations and climatological substitutions for evaporation by station and month.
4. Check all table values, anomaly calculations, percentages, figure numbering, units, and station labels.
5. Ensure that public figures do not expose sensitive station coordinates or unpublished raw datasets.
