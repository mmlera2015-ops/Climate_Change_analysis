# Methodology

## Climate Projection Framework

This project presents climate projection outputs based on the Coupled Model Intercomparison Project Phase 6 (CMIP6).

## Scenario-Based Assessment

The analysis compares selected CMIP6 scenario pathways to illustrate a range of possible future climate conditions. Scenario results are conditional projections, not forecasts of a single certain future.

## Study Area

The displayed diagrams relate to Bushehr and the northern Persian Gulf region, as identified in the accompanying project PDF.

## Interpretation

When interpreting the diagrams, consider:

- The climate variable and its units.
- The scenario pathway and projection period.
- The spatial representativeness of the model output.
- Model uncertainty and differences among climate models.
- The distinction between projected values, anomalies, and observed climatology.

## Data Management and Reproducibility

The public repository contains selected figures and explanatory documentation only. Original datasets and files containing detailed station metadata or coordinates are intentionally excluded.

A reproducible workflow and code documentation can be added when the corresponding scripts are reviewed and cleared for public release.
